One order, start to finish

Type it once. Everything else follows.

Follow one example order through a small business. Every team works from the same record, so nobody copies anything from one system to another.

  1. 01 · Sales

    A customer says yes

    Your customer accepts a quote online, or your rep marks it won. That's the only time anyone types the order.

    Quote Q-0142 · Northwind Supply, Ottawa · 40 cases × $38.50 = $1,540.00
  2. 02 · Inventory

    Stock is set aside

    The 40 cases are reserved the moment the quote is accepted. If you're running low, your buyer gets a reorder suggestion.

    52 on hand → 40 reserved → 12 left · below reorder point of 20
  3. 03 · Invoicing

    The invoice goes out with the right tax

    GST, HST, QST or PST is picked from where the customer is. The invoice goes out in English or French, whichever they prefer.

    Subtotal $1,540.00 · HST Ontario 13% $200.20 · Total $1,740.20
  4. 04 · Accounting

    The books update themselves

    Sales, receivables and HST payable are posted as it happens. Your accountant sees the same numbers you do.

    Dr Receivables 1,740.20 · Cr Sales 1,540.00 · Cr HST payable 200.20
  5. 05 · Reports

    You see cash and margin today

    Margin on the order and your cash forecast are up to date, without anyone opening a spreadsheet.

    Margin on this order $576.00 (37.4%) · cash forecast updated

Questions

What business owners ask us most, from what it runs on to how we move your data.

Ask us something
Is this one more tool to learn?

It's meant to replace several. Sales, stock, purchasing, projects, invoicing and the books live in one system, so you have fewer logins and fewer places where numbers can disagree. Where replacing a tool doesn't make sense, such as your payroll provider or your Shopify store, we connect to it.

What software is it built on?

Odoo Community, the open-source edition of Odoo, business software used by millions of people around the world. We host it, set it up for Canada, keep it updated and support it. Done & Done is independent and not affiliated with Odoo S.A.

How do you move my data in?

Our team does it with you. We bring across customers, suppliers, products and your full transaction history from QuickBooks or Xero, contacts and deals from your CRM, and whatever lives in spreadsheets. We run old and new side by side for a week and switch over once the numbers match.

Where is my data stored?

In Canada, with a Canadian-owned hosting provider. Backups are kept in Canada too.

Does this replace my accountant?

No. Your accountant gets their own login, so they can see what they need without you sending files back and forth.

What will it cost?

We're setting prices with our pilot businesses, as a simple monthly plan per company in Canadian dollars with a few users included. Write to us and we'll share where we are.
